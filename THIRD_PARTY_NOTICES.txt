Dewdrop third-party assets

Product photographs
The photographs in web/assets/products are sourced from official brand stores
and the catalog's retailers. Copyright remains with the respective rights holders;
these images are not covered by the MediaPipe license below.
web/data/product-images.json records each product's source page, original image
URL, local path, and SHA-256 checksum. Images may be resized or recompressed for
the offline catalog. Source links and any variant limitations appear in the app's
product details. No generated product illustrations are used for catalog photos.

Reproducibility
web/vendor/mediapipe/manifest.json records the exact npm package SHA-512 and each
bundled file's SHA-256 and byte length, including the model and license.
Run node scripts/setup-vision.mjs --check for an entirely offline integrity check.
Run node scripts/setup-vision.mjs --download to restore missing/changed assets
from the recorded official URLs. It verifies downloads before writing them.
The application and Android build should package these assets unchanged.

K-pop style reference photographs
The thirteen event/airport/performance photographs in web/assets/looks are bundled as editorial
style references under CC BY 3.0, with original bytes preserved. Each photograph
has its creator, source page, license URL, dimensions, and SHA-256 recorded in
web/data/kpop-looks.json. Full per-photo attribution is bundled in
web/assets/looks/ATTRIBUTION.txt and shown with source/license links in the app.
Region views crop the displayed source photograph; they do not change the stored
image. Style notes and product-category alternatives are editorial suggestions.
Separate sourced campaign links do not identify products worn in an event photo.
Image licenses do not establish artist endorsement or a model release.
